About Sea Atlas

Sea Atlas is an independent, community-driven web interface designed to reimagine the way players interact with the Star Atlas ecosystem. By translating on-chain telemetry and fleet operations into an isometric naval framework, Sea Atlas provides an intuitive graphical visualization layer for SAGE (Star Atlas Golden Era).

Key Features & Architecture

  • On-Chain Data Integration: Sea Atlas connects directly to the Solana blockchain to read real-time player profile data, fleet positions, and sector coordinates from the underlying SAGE smart contracts.
  • Isometric Tactical Display: Rather than relying purely on text-heavy dashboards or abstract menus, Sea Atlas maps fleet activities onto a custom isometric naval environment, offering an immediate and engaging sense of spatial awareness.
  • Asset & Resource Oversight: Track your active fleets, monitor resource consumption rates, and keep an eye on maintenance logistics through a streamlined and cohesive user interface.
  • Web-First & Mobile-Oriented Design: Built with modern web technologies, the platform is optimized for seamless performance across desktop browsers, laying the groundwork for future mobile accessibility.

Our Vision

While Sea Atlas currently serves as a visual and operational skin layer for SAGE, it is designed with a broader goal in mind: expanding the reach of the Star Atlas ecosystem. By bridging the gap between raw blockchain data and immersive graphical gameplay, Sea Atlas aims to introduce novel off-chain tactical features, custom fleet encounters, and alternative game modes—bringing new dimensions of strategy and accessibility to players worldwide.
